## Closest to HKU: Pok Fu Lam to The Peak
Start in Pok Fu Lam Country Park and pass Pok Fu Lam Reservoir on a gentle slope up to The Peak, then walk the Lugard Road loop for city views; for sunset, climb High West. The Peak Circle Walk (Lugard Road and Harlech Road) is mostly flat and fully paved, takes under an hour, and its start can be reached by bus, taxi or the Peak Tram.

## More beginner routes (Tourism Board picks)
Hong Kong Island: Dragon's Back (moderate fitness, about 3–4 hours, little shade in the first half, ends at Big Wave Bay where you can swim). New Territories East: Lau Shui Heung and Hok Tau Reservoirs, Bride's Pool Nature Trail, Lobster Bay Country Trail, the High Island Reservoir Geo Trail. New Territories West: Shing Mun Reservoir (easy, with the War Relics Trail), Tai Mo Shan (the summit is currently closed), Tai Lam Country Park (sweet gum woods in autumn and winter). Lantau: Ngong Ping. Outlying islands: Lamma Island Family Trail (about 7 km, 3–4 hours). Route lengths, difficulty and facilities can be filtered by "difficulty" or "duration" on the AFCD Enjoy Hiking website.

## Before you set out
Plan your route from reliable sources, get to know the path and judge whether it suits your fitness. Check the weather: don't go out in typhoons, rainstorms or strong winds, and shorten your walk or turn back if the weather turns. Inexperienced hikers shouldn't hike alone. Bring a hat, raincoat, map, hiking poles, enough water and food, a torch, first aid supplies and a charged phone.

## On the trail
Follow the signs and warnings and don't take shortcuts; keep away from cliff edges and don't climb rocks; don't pick or eat wild fruit, drink stream water, or swim in reservoirs or at beaches without lifeguards; light fires only in designated places; take your rubbish with you. You can download the Security Bureau's "Safeguard HK" or the police "HKSOS" app and turn on location tracking to help rescuers.

## Emergencies
Note the number of the nearest distance post (for example M001) and call 999 or 112, or use the nearest emergency phone. Some country areas have no mobile signal. Hong Kong Observatory: 1878 200; AFCD hotline: 2708 8885.
