## Who needs an extension
Full-time non-local students should apply to extend their student visa if their permitted stay ends before their programme does, or if they need to extend their period of study. Keeping a valid visa is the student's own responsibility; continuing to study without one is illegal. Part-time students should apply for a new visa instead of an extension; if your visa has already expired, you must apply for a new entry visa.

## Steps (overseas students)
1. Within 6 weeks before your current visa expires, request a support letter from the Admissions Office through HKU's Student Visa Application System. With complete documents, the letter is usually ready online within 5 working days; applications submitted too early are put on hold.
2. With the support letter, apply for the extension online directly with the Immigration Department's Extension Section within 4 weeks before your visa expires.
3. You must be in Hong Kong when you submit the application and when you collect the extended visa. The Immigration Department usually takes about 2–3 weeks.

Mainland students follow the process on HKU's "Mainland Students – Extension of Stay" page.

## Documents to upload (support letter)
Copies of your HKU student card; your Hong Kong identity card (if applicable); the personal details page of your passport / travel document; your visa (e-Visa) and your latest landing slip.

## Temporary extension for graduates
Students who have completed all graduation requirements and are only waiting for their results can apply on the Immigration Department website, within 4 weeks before their permitted stay ends, for a "temporary extension of stay as a student"; no HKU support letter is needed. During a temporary extension you may not work without the Immigration Department's prior approval, and you must declare that you have completed your programme and will apply under the Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG).

## Work restrictions
Student visa holders may not work in Hong Kong unless the Immigration Department has issued a No Objection Letter. Breaching your conditions of stay is a serious immigration offence. Enquiries: HKU Admissions Office, https://www.studentvisa.hku.hk/contact-us. The Immigration Department may change its requirements and may refuse applications.
