## What an Octopus card is
Octopus is a contactless stored-value card for public transport and everyday small payments. Types include on-loan Octopus cards (Adult, Elder and Child; anonymous or Personalised), sold Octopus products, bank co-branded cards, and mobile Octopus on iPhone / Apple Watch, Huawei Wallet and Samsung Pay.

## Buying a Standard Octopus
A Standard (on-loan) Octopus has a refundable HK$50 deposit. The Adult card comes with HK$150 stored value and no fare concessions; a Personalised Octopus comes with HK$130 stored value plus a HK$20 handling fee. You can buy one at most MTR station Customer Service Centres (except some stations such as Airport, Lo Wu, Lok Ma Chau and Racecourse). An Adult card with no add-value or payment for three years in a row becomes an "inactive Octopus" and may be charged an administration fee; return it for the deposit and remaining value if you no longer need it.

## MTR Student Travel Scheme: who qualifies
MTR defines a "student" as someone aged 12–25 studying a full-time day programme at a recognized institution in Hong Kong (you can apply until you turn 26, based on your age on the day you apply). A full-time day programme generally means at least 20 hours a week for 30 weeks or more a year. MTR's pages do not set out arrangements for university or non-local students specifically; HKU's Office of Global Engagement suggests checking eligibility with your Faculty Office first, or asking an MTR Customer Service Centre.

## MTR Student Travel Scheme: how to apply
You need a Personalised Octopus or Personalised mobile Octopus with your photo, set with "Student Status", to get MTR student fares; single journey tickets have no student discount. Apply online on the MTR website or with a paper form. A new application (with a new Personalised Octopus) costs HK$90 (HK$50 deposit, MTR HK$20 administration fee and Octopus HK$20 service fee; first-time applicants get a HK$20 fare rebate); after approval the new card is posted in about 4–6 weeks, and you activate it with the Octopus App or at a Customer Service Centre. If you already have a Personalised Octopus with your photo, activation or renewal costs a HK$20 administration fee. Paper forms need your school's stamp and must be submitted within 14 days of stamping.

## Limits
Student Status applies to MTR only (not the Airport Express, MTR Feeder Bus, journeys to or from Lo Wu / Lok Ma Chau, or East Rail Line First Class); other transport may charge adult fares. Student Status is generally valid for 1–3 years and must be renewed before it ends. Report a lost card at once on 2266 2266. Enquiries: MTR hotline 2881 8888, Octopus hotline 2266 2222.
