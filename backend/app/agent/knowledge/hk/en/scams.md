## Students are a high-risk group
The HKU handbook warns that scams targeting students have risen sharply in recent years, and many have lost large sums. It recommends HKU CEDARS's anti-scam resources (https://wp2.cedars.hku.hk/anti-scam/) and the police Anti-Deception Coordination Centre's scam alerts, joining anti-scam talks, and staying alert to suspicious requests.

## SIM cards and personal details
OFCA warns: don't register SIM cards for unknown people with your own details, and don't buy "pre-registered" pre-paid SIM cards from unknown sources. Report suspected crimes to the police.

## About HK Mate
HK Mate never asks for your passwords, verification codes or card numbers in a chat. If you get a suspicious call or message, check the latest alerts on the Anti-Deception Coordination Centre website or ask the police.
