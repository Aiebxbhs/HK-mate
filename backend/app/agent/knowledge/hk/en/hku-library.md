## The libraries
Main Library; Fung Ping Shan Library (Chinese library, mainly Chinese and Japanese books, on Levels 4–6 of the Main Library); Tin Ka Ping Education Library (education); Ko Wong Wai Ching Wendy Fine Arts Digital Library (fine arts); Dental Library; Lui Che Woo Law Library (law); Yu Chun Keung Medical Library (medicine); Music Library; plus Special Collections, Media Services, Library 24 / Library 24 Study Hub and the 24-hour Study Room.

## Opening hours
Opening hours vary by date and term. The official "Opening Hours" page lists each library's hours for the day, with separate Holiday Hours and Adverse Weather Arrangements. The website lists Library 24, Library 24 Study Hub and the 24-hour Study Room as open 24 hours. Check the official hours for the day before you go.

## Borrowing rules for postgraduates
Books: 180-day loans, renewable up to 1,080 days, up to 180 items. Interlibrary Loan: 30-day loans, up to 90 days, up to 15 items. Audio-visual materials: 7 or 14 days (depending on the item), up to 6 items. Equipment: same-day return (30 minutes before the library closes), 1 item. Short loans and Reserve materials have shorter loan periods; see the Postgraduates table on the official Borrowing Privileges page.

## Booking discussion rooms and study spaces
Discussion rooms, AV group viewing rooms, Concept and Creation Rooms, computers, Research Carrels, Single Study Rooms and more are booked through the official "Book a Space" page; each facility's rules are on the "Booking Policy" page. Booking is generally first come, first served, but Single Study Rooms and Research Carrels have separate allocation for higher degree students. Most facilities need you to check in on site to activate a booking. Don't leave belongings unattended on a seat; library staff may remove them.

## Printing, copying and scanning
The Main Library and branch libraries have self-service copiers and printers on the uPrint printing quota system, with black-and-white and colour printing; some machines accept Wi-Fi printing from your own computer. Top up your print quota in the HKU App (HKU students and staff) or with Octopus at a value-adding machine. Printer locations by floor, prices and how to scan are on the official "Copy, Print, Scan" page.
