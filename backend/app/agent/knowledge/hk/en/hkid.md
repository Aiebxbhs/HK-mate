## Who must register
Under the Registration of Persons Ordinance, people aged 11 or over who are permitted to stay in Hong Kong for more than 180 days must register for a Hong Kong identity card (unless exempted). People permitted to stay for 180 days or less do not need to register. The HKU handbook says students permitted to stay for more than 180 days must register within 30 days of arrival (or of getting that permission to stay).

## When to apply
New arrivals aged 18 or over who are permitted to stay for more than 180 days must register within 30 days of arrival. If your card is lost or damaged, apply for a replacement at a Registration of Persons Office within 14 days.

## How to apply
1. Book an appointment first: online (www.gov.hk/icbooking), through the Immigration Department mobile app, or by phone on 2598 0888. Appointments can be made up to 96 working days ahead.
2. The application form is accepted electronically only. After booking you can fill it in beforehand at www.gov.hk/icbooking, or use the self-service kiosks at the office on the day.
3. You must apply in person at a Registration of Persons Office: take a ticket, have both thumbprints scanned, have your photo taken, check your details and sign, then meet an officer. You then receive an "Acknowledgement of Application for an Identity Card".

## Documents to bring
New arrivals must bring the originals of: a valid travel document or passport, the entry permit (such as a visa or entry permit), or documents proving their status of stay in Hong Kong. Full requirements are in Immigration Department form ROP 138.

## Fees and collection
First registration is free; replacements for lost or damaged cards are charged. Processing normally takes about 5 working days, and the card can usually be collected the working day after processing is complete. Collect it in person by the date on the acknowledgement (or authorize someone with form ID678). When travelling on the acknowledgement, you must also show a valid travel document.

## Good to know
The HKU handbook reminds students to carry their Hong Kong identity card and passport / travel document as proof of identity while in Hong Kong. Enquiries: Immigration Department, (852) 2824 6111, enquiry@immd.gov.hk.
