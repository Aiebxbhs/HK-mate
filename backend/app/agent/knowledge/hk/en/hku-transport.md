## MTR to the Main Campus
- Station: HKU Station on the MTR Island Line
- Exit: Exit A1 or A2
- How: take the lift at the exit to level FB; it opens onto Upper University Street (Haking Wong Building 5/F)
- Best for: the Main Library and other Main Campus buildings
- Also: level G of the same lift is Exit A1, leading to Pokfulam Road

## MTR to the Centennial Campus
- Exit: Exit C1 (or Exit A2)
- How: leave by Exit C1 onto Pokfulam Road, take the escalator up to the footbridge and cross into the Centennial Campus
- Best for: the Lui Che Woo Law Library, the Music Library and other Centennial Campus buildings
- Accessibility: the lift tower beside Exit C1 links by footbridge to the G/F Central Podium Level of the Centennial Campus
- Via Exit A2: take the lift up to level FB, cross the footbridge, turn right and follow University Street into the Centennial Campus

## Buses and green minibuses
The routes below are listed by starting point by the HKU Faculty of Social Sciences.
- Admiralty buses: 23, 30X, 37A, 40, 40M, 90B
- Admiralty minibuses: 10, 31
- Central buses: 3A, 4, 4X, 7, 23, 30X, 37A, 40, 40M, 71, 90B, 91
- Central minibuses: 22, 22S, 55
- Kowloon buses: 103, 970, 970X, 973
Check stops, frequencies and fares with the operators or the Transport Department.

## Campus shuttle bus: routes
Run by the HKU Estates Office for HKU students and staff only; show your student or staff card when boarding. Routes for 1 September 2026 to 31 May 2027:
- Route 1: Main Campus ⇄ Sassoon Road Campus
- Route 1a: Main Campus → Kennedy Town Centre → Sassoon Road Campus
- Route 2: Sassoon Road Campus circular
- Route 3: Sha Wan Drive morning service
- Night Service: Main Campus → Stanley Ho Sports Centre (some times in term only)
- Interim Sports Facilities Service: Main Campus → Stanley Ho Sports Centre → West Island School (Monday to Sunday)
- Main Campus terminus: Library Extension (behind the Main Building)
- Sassoon Road terminus: the roundabout outside the Jockey Club Building for Interdisciplinary Research (BIR), 5 Sassoon Road

## Campus shuttle bus: fares and times
- Student fare: $2 per trip, with an Octopus set with Student Status
- Night and interim services: Night Service and Interim Sports Facilities Service are $7 per trip
- Payment: Octopus only, no cash
- Service days: Monday to Friday; no service on public and university holidays or under Typhoon Signal No. 8 or a Black Rainstorm Warning; arrangements for Reading Week and similar periods are announced on the official website
- Departure times: timetables posted at each stop, the Estates Office website, or the HKU Shuttle Bus App (with live locations)
- Enquiries: 3917 8214
