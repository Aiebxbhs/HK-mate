## Transcripts, testimonials and student cards
Handled by the Registry's Academic Services Office.
- Transcripts: HKU Portal → SIS Main Menu → Online Applications → Application for Transcript; track progress under Application Record in the same place
- Testimonials: same path, choose Application for Testimonial; for an IANG visa after graduation you need the post-graduation Testimonial
- Verifying your registration: if an organisation gives you a form for the University to confirm your registration, submit a Certification application to the Academic Services Office
- Replacement student card: apply online in SIS Self Services (Report Lost/ Damaged Card); email student card questions to regcard@hku.hk
- Location: Room G-04, Run Run Shaw Building
- Office hours: Monday to Friday 9:00–17:30 (open over lunch), Saturday 9:00–12:30; closed on Sundays, public holidays and University holidays
- Contact: 3917 2433 · asoffice@hku.hk

## Paying tuition fees
Based on the 2025-26 postgraduate handbook.
- Invoice: SIS sends your student invoice to your @connect.hku.hk email; you can also download it in SIS
- Payment: pay using the BillType and Bill Account No. on the invoice, and keep the bank receipt or online payment reference as proof
- Deadline: pay in full by the due date on the invoice; 30 days overdue and your registration is cancelled, and reinstatement costs a surcharge of 10% of the amount owed or $500, whichever is lower
- Can't pay on time: apply to CEDARS for deferred payment as early as possible before the due date, at feedefer@hku.hk
- Refunds: fees paid are not refunded; leaving mid-year still means paying the full year's fees
- Enquiries: Student Unit, Finance and Enterprises Office, Knowles Building 1/F

## Seeing a doctor: University Health Service
- Clinic: 2/F, Meng Wah Complex; preventive, elective and more expensive items are charged
- Appointments: online, by phone on 2549 4686, or at the 2/F clinic; book ahead
- Dental: 3/F, Meng Wah Complex, charged; appointments 3917 2510
- Specialists and hospital care: by referral from a UHS doctor to government specialist clinics or public hospitals (full-time students only, excluding obstetrics)
- Cancelling: dental, physiotherapy, health assessment, travel health and similar appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours ahead, or a penalty is charged
- Other numbers: general enquiries 3917 2501; emergencies during opening hours 3917 1999
- Note: non-local students should buy their own medical insurance to cover specialist and hospital costs; students' family members cannot use UHS
- Email: uhealth@hku.hk

## Counselling: CEDARS Mind and Wellbeing
- Service: confidential, free one-to-one counselling by clinical psychologists and counsellors
- Appointments: call 3917 8388 or email cedars-mwb@hku.hk; a duty counsellor first does a 15–20 minute initial assessment, then a regular session is arranged
- Location: Room 301–323, Main Building
- Office hours: Monday to Thursday 9:00–17:45, Friday 9:00–18:00
- After hours: Hospital Authority 24-hour mental health hotline 2466 7350; The Samaritans 24-hour multilingual hotline 2896 0000
- In immediate danger: call 999 at once or go to the nearest Accident and Emergency department

## Disabilities and special educational needs
- Team: CEDARS Diversity and Inclusion
- Support: academic support, counselling, peer support, special accommodation requests, financial aid
- Registration: medical or other supporting documents are required
- Location: Room 123, 1/F, Main Building
- Contact: 3910 3200 · inclusion@hku.hk

## Housing, non-local students and careers
- Halls: taught postgraduates generally can't get University housing and are advised to rent off campus; CEDARS lists rentals near campus at cedars.hku.hk/offcampushousing. Private housing is expensive, so arrange it before coming to Hong Kong if you can
- Postgraduate housing enquiries: pghousing@hku.hk
- Non-local students: the CEDARS site cedars.hku.hk/wow covers arriving, starting university and settling in; enquiries internationalsupport@hku.hk
- Careers: CEDARS Careers and Employability, 3/F, Meng Wah Complex; 3917 2317 · careers@hku.hk
- CEDARS general office: 3/F, Meng Wah Complex; 3917 2305 · cedars@hku.hk

## Emergencies and safety
- Emergencies on campus: Security Control Centre (24 hours) 3917 2882
- Life in danger: call the police on 999, call an ambulance on 2735 3355, or go to the nearest Accident and Emergency department
- Emergency help on University-arranged trips abroad: call the insurer's 24-hour emergency assistance hotline directly; the number is in HKU Portal: SIS Menu → Financial Services → FEO Info and Hotlines → Finance and Enterprises Office → Insurance Information for Students
- University insurance: the University buys group accident and travel insurance for registered students covering University-organised activities only; private travel is not covered
