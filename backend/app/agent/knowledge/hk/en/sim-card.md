## The basic rule
Since 1 March 2022, all pre-paid (stored-value) and subscription SIM cards must be registered with the user's own identity document before they can be used.

## Information needed
Individual users give their name in English and Chinese, date of birth, Hong Kong identity card number and a copy of the card, and show the original for checking. People without a Hong Kong identity card can register with another valid identity document (such as a passport), but must declare that they have no Hong Kong identity card and have not registered with another document. Hong Kong identity card holders must register with that card. Users under 16 need an adult aged 18 or over to endorse the registration.

## How to register
Insert the pre-paid SIM, turn on mobile data and disconnect from Wi-Fi, then follow the link in the operator's SMS: confirm the number or ICCID, choose the document type, photograph and upload the original document, check the details and confirm the declaration. You get an SMS when it succeeds. Hong Kong identity card holders should register pre-paid SIMs with iAM Smart; otherwise the details are checked by hand and activation is slower. You can also ask staff at the operator's shop to help.

## Limits and warnings
Each operator can register up to 10 pre-paid SIM cards per person; there is no limit for subscription plans. Don't buy "pre-registered" SIM cards from unknown sources, and don't register SIM cards for strangers with your own details; giving false information can be a criminal offence. Operators send registration messages from the sender ID "#SIMREG" and may spot-check registered details; pre-paid SIMs whose users don't cooperate with checks are deregistered.
